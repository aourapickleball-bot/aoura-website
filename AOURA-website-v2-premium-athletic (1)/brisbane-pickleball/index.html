Where to play pickleball in Brisbane

Brisbane has a growing mix of public courts, clubs, indoor venues and organised social sessions. AOURA's niche is deliberately narrower: Friday night pickleball in East Brisbane. That makes it useful for inner-Brisbane players who want an organised session rather than simply finding an empty public court.

Why AOURA is different

AOURA centres the experience around the session itself: the people, the atmosphere, the rotation of games and the reason you want to return next Friday. The brand is social first, with space for competitive play without making every booking feel like a tournament.

Brisbane pickleball for first-timers

New players often overthink equipment, rules and whether they are “good enough.” Start with the basics: comfortable court shoes, water and a willingness to learn. AOURA can provide equipment, while the first-timer guide explains what happens when you arrive.

East Brisbane pickleball

AOURA's flagship sessions run at Malpass Tennis, 1 Hilton Street, East Brisbane. That location gives AOURA a clear local identity while still being accessible from Brisbane's inner suburbs.
