# AOURA Website V2 — Premium Athletic Editorial Direction

V1 had a strong SEO and information foundation but visually leaned too far toward a SaaS/dashboard aesthetic. V2 shifts to oversized editorial typography, full-width campaign sections, black/cream/AOURA-red contrast, pill CTAs, fewer boxes and stronger negative space.

The strongest future upgrade is real AOURA photography: night court hero, motion-blur gameplay, community candids, East Brisbane establishing shots and apparel/editorial details.
