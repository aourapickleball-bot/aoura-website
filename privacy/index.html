Privacy

Privacy notice.

Plain-language information about the data collected through AOURA.com.au.

Email subscriptions

If you submit your email address through the AOURA website, it is sent to Mailchimp for mailing-list management. AOURA uses it to send session updates, community news, collaborations, releases and related AOURA communications.

Your choice

Joining the mailing list is voluntary. Marketing emails should include an unsubscribe option so you can stop receiving them.

Bookings

AOURA session bookings are handled through OpenSports. Information you submit there is handled through that service rather than this website.

External links

The site links to services including OpenSports, Instagram and Google Maps. Those services operate under their own privacy practices.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the AOURA website and its services develop.
