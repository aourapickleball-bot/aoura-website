First time?

Start where you are.

You do not need to know the rules perfectly. You do not need expensive gear. You do not need to bring three friends. You need a booking, sports shoes, water and enough curiosity to step on court.

Check beginner-friendly sessions
Before

Book first.

Check the live OpenSports description and make sure the current session matches your level.

Bring

Shoes + water.

Wear comfortable sports clothing and suitable court shoes. Equipment is available.

Arrive

Say you're new.

Tell the host it is your first time so you can be pointed in the right direction from the start.

What pickleball feels like

Pickleball is easy to begin but gives you plenty to improve. Your first goal is not to look advanced. Learn the basic serve, understand the kitchen, keep score and get comfortable with doubles positioning.

Coming alone

That is normal. AOURA's session structure is designed so people can rotate partners and opponents rather than needing to arrive as a fixed team.

Choosing the right session

This matters. A beginner-friendly night and a competitive rated night are not the same experience. The live OpenSports listing is the source of truth for the current format, so read it before booking.

Your first win

It is not the score. It is leaving the court knowing how the game works, having met a few people, and being comfortable enough to come back.
